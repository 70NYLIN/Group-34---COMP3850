ThriveTogether offers a range of family-focused programs including wellness workshops, community events, and support group sessions. Programs are available throughout the year and are open to all community members.
You can enrol by completing the multi-step enquiry form available on the Contact page. A staff member will follow up within 2–3 business days to confirm your enrolment and provide further information.
Selected workshops and community sessions can be accessed online depending on program availability, participant location, and staff capacity. Please mention online attendance in your enquiry message.
Most ThriveTogether programs are offered free of charge thanks to support from our funders and partners. Where small contributions apply (such as for materials or workshop kits), this will be discussed during enrolment.
All public guides, handbooks, and information packs are available on the Resources page. You can use the search bar or category filters to find what you need, then click the download button on each card.
Yes. Schools, healthcare providers, and community organisations may submit partnership enquiries through the Contact form for collaborative programs, community events, training, or educational resources.
Sessions are delivered in community settings including schools, community hubs, and online where appropriate. We work with each community to find a venue that feels safe and accessible.
Sessions typically combine guided activities, practical resources, and time for questions or discussion. Each program is designed for a specific audience and runs between 60 and 90 minutes depending on the activity.
